Round 3 · mean reversion, revised

Three holes in round 2 reversion, three closures

1 · trend guard

Do not buy a falling mean

Split the 15-sample window in half. If the second half's mean sits more than 0.3% below the first half's, the bot treats the dip as a trend and skips the entry.

The guard applies to entries only. No gate can block a sell.

2 · asymmetric bands

DRFT never bought at all

A symmetric 1.5% band placed nothing. DRFT's moves are upward, so price rarely sits 1.5% below its mean. With no buy there is no position, so no sell either.

Now the bot buys the small frequent dips at 0.6% and sells into the large rare spikes at 1.2%.

3 · DCA ladder

A deeper dip is a stronger signal

Each tranche needs a deeper dip than the last. On ELXR: 0.4%, 0.8%, 1.2% below the mean, 800 shares each. A single shallow dip buys once.

required_deviation(n) = band × (1 + n × step)
tranches_held = position // trade_quantity

Worst case is unchanged

Max tranches and the position cap bound the ladder, so the worst case stays cap × stop-loss. DCA moves the average entry price and how often we reach the cap.

Restart-safe by construction

The bot derives the tranche count from the exchange-reported position. It survives a restart, resets at a session boundary and cannot drift from what we hold.

Never on momentum

Adding on an adverse move inverts a momentum thesis. Momentum already scales in on favourable moves by re-buying while the trend persists.

Round 3 · momentum and pairs, retuned

Wider windows, same slope

NOVA · 10 to 25 samples
1.2%
threshold, from 0.8% on 10 samples

Momentum is (last − first) / first. A wider window measures a larger change for the same trend, so the threshold must scale with it. Slope held at 0.0005 to 0.0008 per sample. Noise grows with √N, trend with N. James p.1 and Derek both ask for the wide lookback here.

CRVX · 10 to 15 samples
0.75%
threshold, slope-preserved from 0.5% / 10

Growth name, faster than NOVA, so a tighter window by the same rule. It keeps its own parameters.

Trailing take-profit closes the exit gap

Momentum's own exit needs the whole 25-sample return to turn negative. The trail arms at +4% from cost and exits on a 1.5% give-back from the peak.

AXON · pairs follow off NOVA

Arm 5 to 10 ticks · exit 0.4% to 1.2%

NOVA prints a new 10-sample high. AXON arms for 10 of its own ticks and buys 400. When NOVA gives back 1.2% from that high, the bot closes AXON. At 0.4% the follower sold on NOVA's first wobble.

This is a correlated long, not a hedge. The exchange is long-only, so AXON stacks on NOVA's exposure. Clarissa: "if we're long both we've doubled a position." The pair shares one $1.2M notional cap, pending orders included, at live marks.

The follower reads the leader's regime. A breakout is a momentum signal on another instrument.

Round 3 · stop-loss and take-profit

Exits run before the strategy is asked

Priya Sundaram: "This system ran eighteen months with no stop-losses and I only found out last month." The bot checks exits on every price event. Cost basis comes from the exchange's avg_cost, so levels survive a restart.

Changed this round

ELXR stop 1.5% to 3%

The tight stop sat inside ELXR's normal range. The bot stopped out, re-entered on the next dip and stopped out again. The wider stop ends that loop.

Exits pay to get out

An exit order concedes 25 bps of spread. A strategy order concedes 5. No gate (spread, liquidity, regime, news) can block an exit.

A symbol with no exits block has no exits. We do not guess at a risk tolerance nobody set.

Round 3 · execution quality · the bot now reads the book

Size to the book

Adeline Loh: "If we're getting lots of partial fills on NOVA, our sizing is too big for the book." The bot caches the SSE book_update feed and consults it before every entry.

Spread filter · how wide

Refuse a wide touch

The bot refuses an entry when the spread exceeds 50 bps (100 bps on DRFT). If the book is older than 5 s it falls back to blind offset pricing instead of trading on old data.

Liquidity filter · how deep

Shrink the clip

The bot cuts the entry size to the shares resting at or better than its limit. Below a floor (NOVA 10, ELXR 20, DRFT 5) it skips the entry.

Our own resting orders are excluded. Crossing one returns 409 SELF_MATCH.

Cooldown
3 s
minimum between orders on one symbol

A persistent signal can no longer stack orders tick after tick.

Regime multiplier · on

Can only shrink a buy

ATR-like volatility and directional efficiency give TREND, RANGE, COMPRESSION, SHOCK or NEUTRAL. Each family maps a regime to a multiplier in [0, 1] on new buys. Sells are never touched.

Enabled in config with 3-sample hysteresis. Thresholds are playbook defaults, not yet tuned on captured windows.

Trading · how a signal becomes an order

A fixed priority order

1

Session closed · halt · stale data

No trading.

2

Stop-loss · trailing TP · take-profit

Forced sell. The strategy is not consulted.

3

Hard limits

Per-symbol cap, correlated-group notional, pending exposure counted.

4

Human override

Pause, reduce-only, HALT button.

5

News directive

Keyword tier at once, LLM tier async. Bias, size multiplier, TTL 120 s.

6

Base strategy signal

Momentum, mean reversion with DCA, pairs, crash-buy. Then spread, liquidity, regime and cooldown gates on entries only.

Nothing lower can weaken anything higher

An LLM view, a dashboard toggle or a strategy cannot bypass a stop, a cap or the kill switch. One test file asserts that no exit path is ever gated.

Long-only, limit-only

Prices in integer cents. One active order per symbol. The bot reconciles an uncertain outcome (504) by idempotency key before it reports success.

Instruments discovered at startup

GET /instruments on boot. Strategies register with a router. There is no if-else chain in main.py.

Round 3 · backtesting

Replay the production strategy code

How it works
  • No candles endpoint exists, so record_feed.py records the live SSE feed and the engine replays it
  • The same build_strategy the live bot uses builds the strategy object. Buy and sell decisions come from the same code
  • A signal on tick t fills against tick t+1. No same-tick fills, no look-ahead
  • A buy fills only if the next price is at or under our limit. Otherwise it is cancelled, like the live one-shot orders
  • --slippage-bps makes the optimism a visible knob
  • Sweep: lookback × threshold, 3 × 3, live config always in the grid. Ranked on total P&L. A winner needs at least 2 fills
Followed the data

ELXR: wider band wins, so the ladder starts at 0.4% and deepens

Every 0.6% row beat every 0.4% row. 0.2% lost. The DCA ladder at 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2% keeps the first clip on ELXR's frequent small dips and fires the later clips only on the deviations the sweep says pay.

DRFT: momentum lost on all 9 combinations

On a thin book our own buys lift the last price, and momentum reads that as its own signal. We switched to spike-and-revert.

AXON: mean reversion placed zero trades

We switched to pairs follow off NOVA.

Overrode the data, on purpose

NOVA: the sweep favoured 5 samples. We use 25.

In a 130-tick clean uptrend the fastest window buys earliest and the rest is unrealised mark-to-market. The gap is $365 on $11k. That sample says nothing about noise rejection in a choppy window, which is what James p.1 and Derek warn about.

Caveats ship with the results
  • One session, 650 ticks, 387 s. Directional only
  • Price-only series: no depth, no partials, no liquidity limit. It flatters large sizes and high turnover
  • The output JSON feeds the dashboard's Backtest page with these caveats attached

AI · built with, and built in

The LLM reasons about the supply chain. It never touches an order.

AI in the product · llm.codetoimpact.sg

Two-tier announcement engine

  • Tier 0, keyword: instant, coarse, on the event-worker thread. It prefers pause or reduce-size over adding exposure
  • Tier 1, LLM (sonnet): own thread, one call per announcement_id, memoised. It returns per-symbol views across the supply-chain web. Sanctions on freight hit AXON directly and ripple to NOVA and CRVX for different reasons. Sophia Tan asked for that reasoning
  • Views under 0.6 confidence are logged, not traded. Hard cap 60 calls per session, about $0.003 a call

Why sonnet

A short structured inference at single-digit calls per window. Cost does not bind, so we took the middle model. Haiku is cheaper. Opus reasons harder if this proves too shallow. The output is validated JSON, never executable.

Trading · strengths and weaknesses

What holds up, and what does not yet

Strengths
  • The bot stands down. Trend guard, regime multiplier and exits each encode a "do not enter" decision
  • A relationship trade. AXON trades NOVA's breakout under a shared cap
  • Sizing follows the book. The liquidity filter and DCA make thin names tradeable
  • Worst case is arithmetic. Cap × stop, per instrument, checked before anything else runs
  • Decisions are testable. The backtester runs the live classes
Weaknesses
  • Long-only exchange. No hedge. "Pairs" is a correlated long and the stop is the only protection in a sell-off
  • The backtest sample is one 6-minute session with no book. Parameters are priors plus one data point
  • Regime thresholds are playbook defaults. The multiplier is on, but nobody has tuned it on captured windows
  • crash_buy is unassigned and conflicts with the news engine's bearish suppression
  • Market-making stays disabled. The inventory-skew bug is known and unfixed
  • Two dashboard sections wait on bot endpoints. The Strategy tab and per-instrument pause and stop levels show placeholders until the bot exposes them over its control API
  • No predictive view. Sophia's forward-looking estimate with a confidence band is not built

Reflection

What we would keep, change, and what we enjoyed

Did well
  • Safety before alpha. Round 1 had no strategy work, and that let rounds 2 and 3 move fast
  • We wrote the reason next to every parameter, so each change is an argument
  • We let the sandbox falsify us. DRFT momentum and AXON mean reversion measurably did nothing, so we dropped them

Could be better
  • Record more feed earlier. The backtester arrived before the data it needed
  • Tune the regime thresholds on captured windows. James called page 3 "the one that matters"
  • Ship the conversational panel on the MCP layer that has been ready since round 2
  • Fewer config commits by hand. The key leak cost a rotation
Enjoyed
  • Reading twelve interviews that disagreed and having to pick
  • Watching the tape of the team leading the board and turning it into a strategy the same afternoon
  • Seeing a 0-trade instrument start trading because we made the band asymmetric
  • Building something we would put in front of a desk
